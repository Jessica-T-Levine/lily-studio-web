Digital Products

Claude AI Skills & Companions

Lily Studio sells digital skill files (.skill and .zip format) through Etsy, Gumroad, and Agensi. These files are downloaded to your device and loaded into Claude AI by you.

What we collect

Lily Studio does not collect any personal data through our skill files. The files contain instructions for Claude AI — they do not phone home, track usage, or transmit any information. Once downloaded, the file lives entirely on your device.

What the platforms collect

When you purchase a skill, the platform you buy from (Etsy, Gumroad, or Agensi) processes your payment and may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Lily Studio receives only the information necessary to fulfill your order — typically your name, email address, and what you purchased.

How to contact us

If you have questions about how your data is handled, email us at support@thelilystudio.com.
