Privacy Policy

Calming Water: Baby & Toddler
Effective Date: April 5, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Calming Water: Baby & Toddler is developed and operated by Jessica Levine (“Lily Studio”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our app, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

We built this app with privacy in mind. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any information that identifies you personally. We do not require an account. We do not show ads.

By using Calming Water: Baby & Toddler, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device (never transmitted to Lily Studio)

We store the following information locally on your device only:

  • User preferences — volume level, mute state, selected ambient sound, selected waterscape, haptics setting, and Baby Lock setting. Stored using AsyncStorage (standard iOS local storage).
  • Trial and purchase state — your free trial start date and your purchase entitlement status. Stored using SecureStore, which uses the iOS Keychain for secure local storage.

This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server we operate.

Information collected by third-party services

We use three third-party services that collect limited, anonymous data as described in Section 8. None of these services collect your name, email, location, or any information that identifies you personally.

3. How We Use Information

The preferences stored on your device are used solely to provide the app experience — restoring your settings between sessions and tracking whether your trial or purchase is active.

Data collected by our third-party services (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring) is used to understand how the app is used, identify crashes, and monitor app performance so we can fix problems and improve the experience.

We do not use any data for advertising. We do not sell any data to any party, ever.

4. California Residents — CCPA

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your personal information

We do not sell personal information. The only data we process is anonymous diagnostic data through Firebase (Google), as described in Section 8.

To exercise your rights or ask questions, contact us at support@thelilystudio.com.

5. UK and EEA Residents — UK GDPR / GDPR

If you are located in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing anonymous diagnostic data through Firebase is our legitimate interest in maintaining a stable, functional application.

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any data associated with you. Because we do not collect personally identifiable information and have no accounts system, we have no way to link Firebase diagnostic data to a specific individual.

For questions about your rights, contact us at support@thelilystudio.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

Calming Water: Baby & Toddler is designed for use by parents and caregivers with infants and toddlers. The app is rated 4+ and is not directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require any input from a child — all settings and purchases are handled by the adult using the device.

Our use of Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collects only anonymous, non-identifying diagnostic data from the device. No information specific to a child is collected.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information about a child, please contact us at support@thelilystudio.com and we will take immediate steps to address it.

This app complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

7. Data Security

Sensitive data stored on your device (trial state and purchase entitlement) is stored in the iOS Keychain via SecureStore, which is encrypted at rest and protected by iOS security. This data never leaves your device.

Anonymous diagnostic data transmitted to Google (Firebase) is handled according to Google’s security standards. See Google’s Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

No data is transmitted to any server operated by Lily Studio.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each is described accurately below.

RevenueCat

RevenueCat processes in-app purchases and verifies subscription and lifetime purchase entitlements. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat communicates with Apple to confirm the transaction. RevenueCat receives your Apple purchase receipt for this purpose. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or any personal information beyond what Apple’s purchase system provides.
Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Firebase Analytics (Google)

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous data about how the app is used — including app opens, session length, and general usage patterns. This data does not include your name, email address, location, or any information that personally identifies you. It is used solely to understand how the app is being used so we can improve it.
Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google. Google’s Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports when the app encounters an error. A crash report includes the device type, iOS version, app version, and a technical stack trace of what the app was doing when it crashed. No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports. Crash collection is disabled in development and test builds — only production builds send crash reports.
Firebase Crashlytics data is processed by Google. Google’s Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Performance Monitoring (Google)

Firebase Performance Monitoring collects anonymous data about app performance — including app start time and network request performance. No personally identifiable information is collected. Performance data collection is disabled in development and test builds — only production builds send performance data.
Firebase Performance Monitoring data is processed by Google. Google’s Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

The current version of this policy is always available at thelilystudio.com/privacy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us:
