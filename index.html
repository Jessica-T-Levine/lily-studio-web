The Story

In January 2026, I decided to build a software company. I had never written a line of code in my life.

Lily Studio started as a bet. Could someone with zero technical background — no CS degree, no developer friends, no roadmap — build real apps that real people want to use?

The answer, it turns out, is yes. With Claude as a partner, a lot of stubbornness, and a commitment to building in public, Lily Studio ships iOS apps, Claude AI skills, and digital tools that are elegant, useful, and made with care.

Nothing here was handed to us. Everything here was figured out.
