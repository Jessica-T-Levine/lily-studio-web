About

Lily Studio is an independent development studio founded by Jessica — a mom who couldn't find what she wanted for her child, so she figured out how to make it. From that single app was born an entire development studio named after her daughter, who inspired it all.

Lily Studio is founded on the idea of simply getting what you need without the distractions you don't. Every app we ship is something we'd want in our own hands. We build for the individual user — we want each user to feel like we made something just for them.
