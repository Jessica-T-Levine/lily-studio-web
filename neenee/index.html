Lily’s Bun Nee Nee

Hop a very round bunny around a garden that never ends, and munch carrots.

Lily’s Bun Nee Nee
Click the game to play with your keyboard
a tiny game by Lily Studio
Play it like an app: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Keep the carrots in the corner full: they are her tummy, and she gets slow and sleepy when they run out. Tap anywhere and she hops there. Tap a carrot and she hops over, pulls it up and munches it. Hold the mouse button, the trackpad click or the space bar (or the hop button on a phone or tablet) and she hops forward, faster and bigger the longer you hold, until she's at full speed. Move the pointer left or right of the middle, or use the left/right arrows, to steer her. Let go and she slows to a stop. On a computer, click the game once so the keys work too. Leave her alone for a bit and she finds her own fun.
