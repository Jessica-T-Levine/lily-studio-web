Hop a tiny, very round bee across giant flowers in a meadow that never ends.

Click the game to play with your keyboard
Play it like an app: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Press a flower (or anywhere) and she turns that way. Hold to wind up (longer = farther), let go to hop. Land on a bud to make it bloom. Every bloom adds pollen (she can carry 10). Press Fly to the hive any time: she flies home from wherever she is. With pollen, she goes inside and drops it all off; with none, she dances with her friends on the step. Your score is all the pollen she has delivered, and after a visit she stays at home by the hive. Lost? Fly home! Tap the game (or press space) any time during the trip to skip straight home. On a phone or tablet you can also use the arrow and fly buttons. On a computer: click the game once, then the left/right arrows turn her, and holding the space bar (or the up or down arrow) winds up. Let go to hop. While she's flying, hold again to pause her in the air and look around; let go and she carries on. Press Enter (or H) to fly to the hive.
